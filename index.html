How to Prevent Throttling by Your Internet Provider

Some internet providers in Qatar may slow down streaming traffic. SubTV IP recommends two simple settings to avoid this:

  1. Use Cloudflare DNS – change your device’s DNS to 1.1.1.1. This often stops throttling.
  2. Set buffer / cache to 5,000 ms – in your IPTV player app, adjust the buffer size. A larger buffer gives the stream a head start to load smoothly.

These steps are general setup knowledge and not specific to any provider. They work on most IPTV setups.
